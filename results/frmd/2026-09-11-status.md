## Native history and physical execution

Thirty actual raw episodes were prepared: ten each from PatternLock, RouteStick,
and VideoPlaceButton, selected by numeric episode index independently of outcomes.
Their seeds match the benchmark training metadata: this is a **development pilot**,
not held-out task generalization. The prepared catalog has 4,992 execution samples
and 15,167 frozen visual feature frames. The manifest contains 60 k=3/7 cases plus
30 clean k=0 cases. Native tokendrop compression recomputes causal pixel scores and
chronological positions over real concatenated histories. A real prefix matched
all four upstream history arrays exactly on GPU.

The first k=3 case from each task completed paired two-step repair and offline
persistence evaluation. PatternLock and RouteStick rolled back both edits;
VideoPlaceButton recovery was +0.001778 and mean available-horizon persistence
was -0.001385. This teacher view uses provenance-selected relevant histories;
learned retrieval quality has not been evaluated.

Each of these three cases also completed six physical conditions × eight matched
noise replicas from identical simulator fingerprints. All chunks remained ongoing,
so zero terminal successes are not counted as episode failures. FRMD and its
interfered baseline made zero subgoal advancement; the PatternLock teacher made
mean advancement 0.375 (three of eight chunks). There is no observed FRMD physical
benefit in these three cases. Corrected completion-cursor measurements, source
alignment errors, controls, and full results are retained.
