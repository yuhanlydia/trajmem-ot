## Second mechanism and physical evidence

The released frame-sampling checkpoint passed SHA-256/ZIP CRC verification. Its
native encoder matched all four upstream history arrays exactly in six short/long
prefix checks across three tasks. Both models reuse the same prepared data and
repair core; they are two memory mechanisms within the same VLA family.

Frame-sampling first-case FRMD recovery/persistence was 0/0 for PatternLock,
+0.000112/+0.000669 for RouteStick, and +0.002968/+0.001296 for VideoPlaceButton.
Full controls and physical results are in [the second-model report](2026-09-11-second-model-and-live/README.md).

In these three frame-sampling physical cases, PatternLock averaged 0.75 subgoal
advancement per chunk under every condition; the other two tasks averaged zero.
FRMD-minus-baseline advancement was zero. In the initial tokendrop three cases,
FRMD/baseline advancement was zero while the PatternLock teacher averaged 0.375.
All chunks remained ongoing: zero terminal successes are not episode failures.
Physical starting fingerprints matched and raw-source joint alignment errors are
recorded. Task completion uses the corrected authoritative completion cursor.

## Live RGB execution and remaining work

A process-local NVIDIA EGL Vulkan ICD resolved the GLX entrypoint failure without
changing drivers. SAPIEN and all three task environments returned valid RGB with
original visual geometry. CPU tensor readback keeps the benchmark and policy in
their separate pinned Python environments.

The first live launch exposed a virtualenv-path bug; resolving Python's symlink
bypassed its installed packages. A failing-then-passing regression and a successful
real-policy retry verify the correction. In the retry, initial physical and
RGB/state fingerprints matched. Query two encoded 20 newly executed frames and
carried the repair onto 505/512 surviving tokens (applied norm 0.901500 → 0.898497).
Both branches remained ongoing with zero advancement. This establishes the live
update/edit path, not a physical performance advantage.
